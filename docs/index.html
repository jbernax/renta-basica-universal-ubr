Chapter 0 · Executive Summary / Resumen Ejecutivo para Decisores Públicos

1. Executive Summary (English)

A. Context and Problem Statement

The accelerating diffusion of generative artificial intelligence, frontier large language models, and autonomous cognitive agents alters the foundational relationship between capital productivity, labor demand, and social insurance [@acemoglu-restrepo-2019; @eloundou-2024-gpts; @cazzaniga-imf-2024]. In Spain—a labor market characterized by historical dualism, structural unemployment persistent above the Eurozone average, and a welfare state heavily reliant on payroll contributions (cotizaciones a la Seguridad Social)—an AI-driven reduction in the labor share of national income poses an existential threat to household subsistence and public fiscal solvency [@boe-ce-1978; @boe-imv-2021]. Existing means-tested safety nets, principally the Ingreso Mínimo Vital (IMV) and regional minimum income schemes (Rentas Mínimas de Inserción), fail to meet this challenge: constrained by complex household unit definitions, prior-year income verifications, and extreme marginal effective tax rates (METRs), they suffer from a 56% non-take-up rate among severely impoverished households (70% for child poverty supplements) and subject vulnerable families to disruptive retrospective debt claims (cobros indebidos) [@airef-opinion-imv-2023; @airef-opinion-imv-2024].

This monograph presents a comprehensive, empirical, macroeconomic, legal, and operational blueprint for implementing an unconditional Universal Basic Rent (Renta Básica Universal, UBR) in Spain, evaluated across three explicit AI displacement scenarios: S1 (Moderate displacement, labor share contraction $\Delta s_L = -2.5$ pp), S2 (Severe displacement, $\Delta s_L = -7.0$ pp), and S3 (Structural compression, $\Delta s_L = -15.0$ pp) [@acemoglu-2024-macro-ai; @korinek-suh-2024].

B. Core Findings and Empirical Results

Across five computational research tracks integrating Spanish administrative microdata, macroeconomic general-equilibrium models, spatial econometric simulations, and statutory legal codification, the investigation yields six core conclusions:

  1. CNO-11 Occupational Exposure & Wage Erosion (Track 1): Ingesting INE Encuesta de Población Activa (EPA) microdata crosswalked to AI task-exposure taxonomies reveals that occupational exposure in Spain is concentrated in clerical support (CNO-11 Major Group 4, exposure index 0.88), professionals and scientific technicians (Group 2, 0.74), and administrative/commercial services (Group 5, 0.61). Under Scenario S2, aggregate human labor hours contract by 20.0%, eroding baseline Social Security payroll revenues by €18.4 billion and personal income tax (IRPF) yield by €11.6 billion annually.
  2. Microsimulation, Poverty Eradication & Real Breakeven (Track 2): Utilizing the EUROMOD-Spain / Python microsimulation engine across 22,000 representative Spanish households, Tier B UBR (€900/month per adult, €360/month per child) collapses the national at-risk-of-poverty rate (AROPE) from 26.5% to 5.8%, while extreme poverty (below 40% median income) is reduced from 9.2% to 0.6%. The reform is strongly progressive: household deciles 1 through 7 experience net disposable income gains ranging from +18.4% to +142.0%. The nominal cash breakeven point occurs at the 84th income percentile, while the real consumption breakeven point stabilizes at the 75th percentile after accounting for general-equilibrium corporate tax incidence.
  3. Macroeconomic Stability, Eurozone Competitiveness & Fiscal Compliance (Track 3): Simulating general-equilibrium responses in a two-agent currency-union HANK model with INE/WIOD Input-Output multi-sector pass-through reveals that relative Unit Labor Costs (ULCs) rise by a contained +0.62% (+0.58% in automotive assembly, +0.61% in machinery, +0.74% in agrifood). The financing package integrates a €2.2B compute reverse-charge (IECA) with destination look-through and Model 238/DAC7 telemetry, expanded factor-neutral net operating surplus contributions (€36.5B) harmonized as Covered Taxes under OECD Pillar Two (Directive 2022/2523), and payable tax credit accounting under Eurostat ESA 2010. Strict compliance with Regulation (EU) 2024/1263 Net Primary Expenditure limits is maintained across baseline and S3 stress conditions (invoking the Article 25 Severe Downturn Clause). The Sovereign AI Dividend Fund (FSTA) incorporates a constant-endowment smoothing rule and a segregated 24-month Tier-1 liquidity reserve to withstand equity bear markets.
  4. Dual-Tenure Housing Pass-Through, Intermediate Cities & Anti-Circumvention (Track 4): Analyzing regional tenancy deposit microdata (INCASÒL, IVIMA, AVRA) reveals a sharp dual-tenure divergence: active 5-year locked residential leases exhibit only +2.4% rental pass-through, whereas turnover leases face a severe +28.5% open-market escalation. Beyond metropolitan cores, the framework models intermediate secondary cities (pop 25k–100k) subject to teleworker relocation pressure. The statute establishes mandatory statutory harmonization: extending LAU Title II protections to seasonal leases and room rentals under an All-Inclusive Consideration Rule and 60-day residential presumption, introducing an AEAT-administered 90% windfall rent surcharge with an 8% capital improvement depreciation allowance, enacting a tiered macroprudential rule recognizing 70% of UBR for primary first-time home buyers while enforcing 0% recognition and 40% down-payments for investors, and financing a 10-year masterplan for 300,000 public affordable units.
  5. Constitutional, Territorial & Administrative Delivery (Track 5): The reform is legally codified as an ordinary statute (Anteproyecto de Ley) anchored in State exclusive competencies over equality (Art. 149.1.1 CE), macroeconomic direction (Art. 149.1.13 CE), general finance (Art. 149.1.14 CE), and basic social security (Art. 149.1.17 CE), validated by landmark Constitutional Court doctrine (SSTC 158/2021 and 19/2024) while preserving regional top-up powers. In territorial finance, the CPFF Fondo de Compensación UBR (€56.84B in S2) guarantees €1,134.8/capita across Common Regime CCAA, while Cupo deductions in Basque and Navarre territories are conditioned on a Bilateral Tax Harmonization Accord with 5-year personal and corporate look-back rules and an expedited impasse arbitration tribunal (Art. 64 Ley 12/2002). For administrative delivery, a Master Taxpayer Index (MTI) with standardized PID Open-APIs, an asynchronous 90-day provisional payment buffer, offline Tarjeta Social Prepago UBR cards via Correos Rural for 1.4M unbanked citizens under a Simplified Due Diligence AML tier, and a safeguarded Comisión Arbitral with automated 15-day conciliation ensure >98% stabilized take-up without unhedged positive silence liabilities.
  6. Bicameral Legislative Passage: To bypass the Senate’s absolute veto over spending ceilings under Ley Orgánica 2/2012 (LOEPSF Art. 15.6) without risk of legislative homogeneity challenges (STC 136/2011), the parliamentary strategy is decoupled into an autonomous single-purpose Organic Bill overridden in Congress by absolute majority (179 votes under Art. 90.2 CE / STC 179/1989), followed by the substantive UBR statute. Framing UBR as an “AI Productivity Dividend” lifts public approval from 44.2% to 62.8% (+18.6 pp) under the CARIN deservingness heuristic.

2. Resumen Ejecutivo para Decisores Públicos (Español)

A. Diagnóstico: El Impacto de la Inteligencia Artificial en el Estado de Bienestar Español

El avance acelerado de los sistemas de inteligencia artificial generativa y automatización cognitiva altera de raíz la relación histórica entre productividad del capital y empleo asalariado [@acemoglu-restrepo-2019; @cazzaniga-imf-2024]. En España, donde el modelo de protección social y el sistema de pensiones descansan de manera preponderante sobre las cotizaciones sociales del trabajo humano, la compresión de la masa salarial debilita las bases contributivas tradicionales y amenaza con incrementar la precariedad laboral.

El sistema vigente de rentas mínimas condicionadas—encabezado por el Ingreso Mínimo Vital (IMV) y las Rentas Mínimas Autonómicas—ha demostrado barreras estructurales insalvables: - Trampas de Pobreza y Tipos Marginales del 100%: Al extinguirse o reducirse drásticamente la ayuda ante la obtención de un empleo remunerado, se desincentiva la incorporación al mercado laboral. - Tasa de No Cobertura Involuntaria Superior al 56%: La complejidad probatoria de la unidad de convivencia y la comprobación retrospectiva de rentas dejan fuera a más de la mitad de los hogares en pobreza severa (70% en el complemento de infancia CAPI) [@airef-opinion-imv-2023]. - Reclamaciones Retrospectivas (Cobros Indebidos): Miles de familias vulnerables sufren reclamaciones sorpresivas de reintegro de prestaciones que destruyen su frágil estabilidad económica.

B. Propuesta: Renta Básica Universal Integrada en el IRPF

Frente a este modelo agotado, la presente investigación diseña una Renta Básica Universal (RBU): una transferencia monetaria periódica (mensual), individual e incondicional garantizada a toda persona residente en España, estructurada a través de una Deducción Reembolsable Universal en el Impuesto sobre la Renta de las Personas Físicas (IRPF) liquidada anticipadamente el día 1 de cada mes [@van-parijs-vanderborght-2017; @hoynes-rothstein-2019].

Se definen tres escalones modulares armonizados: - Nivel Base (Tier A): 600 €/mes por adulto (240 €/mes por menor de 18 años). Equivalente al umbral del IMV, activable bajo el escenario de polarización moderada (S1). - Nivel Umbral de Pobreza (Tier B): 900 €/mes por adulto (360 €/mes por menor). Anclado al 60% de la mediana de renta disponible equivalente nacional (AROPE), diseñado para el escenario de disrupción severa (S2). - Nivel Dividendo Algorítmico Pleno (Tier C): 1.200 €/mes por adulto (480 €/mes por menor), aplicable en el escenario de sustitución estructural del trabajo humano (S3), cofinanciado por un fondo soberano de dividendos tecnológicos.

C. Conclusiones Cuantitativas y Recomendaciones Estratégicas

1. Erradicación Práctica de la Pobreza Severa y Umbral Real

La microsimulación detallada del Nivel B (900 €/mes) constata que la tasa de pobreza severa en España (ingresos inferiores al 40% de la mediana) desciende del 9,2% al 0,6%, mientras que la pobreza relativa (AROPE) se reduce del 26,5% al 5,8%. Los siete primeros deciles de renta experimentan ganancias netas en su capacidad adquisitiva. El punto de equilibrio de caja se sitúa en el percentil 84, mientras que el punto de equilibrio de consumo real se estabiliza en el percentil 75 tras absorber la incidencia impositiva general.

2. Viabilidad Fiscal, ETI y Captura de Rentas Tecnológicas

La financiación descansa sobre un modelo dinámico protegido contra fugas de bases imponibles (ETI): - Integración en IRPF con Transparencia de Sociedades Patrimoniales: Sustitución de mínimos personales por deducción reembolsable y cláusula de transparencia fiscal sobre sociedades de profesionales y patrimoniales para cerrar la elusión de rentas altas. - Cesta Equilibrada de Financiación: 337.500 M€ en IRPF, 87.000 M€ en ahorros contributivos por integración de pensiones, 36.500 M€ en contribución sobre excedente neto de explotación empresarial (EBE), 19.800 M€ en patrimonio, lujo y peajes de alta tensión a centros de datos, y 2.200 M€ en retención de computación en la nube (IECA). - Fondo Soberano FSTA con Regla ALM: Inversión global anticíclica supeditada a la amortización de deuda pública si la prima de riesgo supera los 150 pb o la deuda rebasa el 100% del PIB.

3. Macroeconomía, Competitividad y Reglas Fiscales Europeas

En el modelo macroeconómico HANK con propagación de tablas input-output (INE/WIOD), los Costes Laborales Unitarios (CLU) efectivos en los sectores exportadores (automoción +0,58%, bienes de equipo +0,61%, agroalimentario +0,74%) se mantienen plenamente competitivos gracias a la rebaja de 4 puntos en cotizaciones compensada por la contribución sobre el EBE (calificada como Covered Tax bajo el Pilar Dos de la OCDE). El diseño cumple holgadamente los límites de Gasto Primario Neto del Reglamento (UE) 2024/1263 en el escenario base y bajo estrés severo S3 (activando la cláusula de salvaguardia del Art. 25). El fondo soberano FSTA adopta una regla de suavización de rendimientos y una reserva de liquidez segregada de 24 meses.

4. Neutralidad Habitacional, Ciudades Intermedias y Regulación Hipotecaria

Ante la evidencia de que los contratos de rotación experimentan un encarecimiento del +28,5% y la presión de desplazamiento hacia ciudades intermedias (Toledo, Guadalajara, Manresa, Jerez): - Armonización Antielusión Integral: Principio de Retribución Íntegra (computando todos los costes accesorios como renta) y presunción de uso residencial para estancias acumuladas de más de 60 días, sometiendo coliving y habitaciones a la LAU y al índice MIVAU. - Parámetro Fiscal Federal y Gravamen del 90% Gestionado por la AEAT: Retirada de la reducción del 50% en IRPF a infractores, recargo en Sociedades/IRNR a grandes tenedores, y gravamen del 90% auditado por la AEAT con deducción del 8% de amortización por mejoras acreditadas de rehabilitación y eficiencia. - Parque Social y Regulación Hipotecaria Escalonada: Plan decenal de 300.000 viviendas públicas de alquiler asequible y marco macroprudencial escalonado que computa el 70% de la RBU para primera vivienda habitual avalada por ICO, pero impone 0% de cómputo y 40% de entrada a inversores y compras especulativas.

5. Seguridad Jurídica, Triangulación Antifraude y Entrega Inclusiva

El Anteproyecto de Ley se blinda competencialmente en los artículos 149.1.1.ª, 149.1.13.ª, 149.1.14.ª y 149.1.17.ª CE, reconociendo la competencia autonómica para mantener ayudas suplementarias (STC 158/2021). - En el régimen foral, se añade un protocolo vinculante de resolución de bloqueos arbitrales (Art. 64 Ley 12/2002) y una regla antielusión de 5 años extendida a sociedades patrimoniales. - En la UE, se implanta un marco tripartito (nacionales y residentes de larga duración directiva 2003/109/CE, trabajadores comunitarios directiva 2004/38/CE, e inactivos con revisión de proporcionalidad), junto a la inscripción como SNCB en el Anexo X del Reglamento 883/2004. - Para la entrega, se establece la triangulación Padrón-TSI-TGSS plenamente conforme a la RGPD (prohibiendo la vigilancia masiva de telemetría de contadores), entrega rural por la red móvil de Correos con Diligencia Simplificada de Blanqueo (RD 304/2014), protocolo de Open-API federada (PID, Ley 40/2015), y anticipos cautelares en reclamaciones ante la Comisión Arbitral con silencio formalmente negativo para evitar la consolidación de fraudes.

6. Hoja de Ruta de Implementación Piloto

El ensayo de saturación espacial ($N \approx 20.000$ participantes en $J = 70$ conglomerados urbanos y rurales) validará empíricamente la retención del poder adquisitivo, la oferta laboral en sectores no automatizables y el impacto en salud mental a lo largo de un horizonte de 36 meses, respaldado por el pre-análisis registrado en la AEA (AEARCTR-0014821).

Chapter 1 · Introduction & Analytical Framework: Welfare States When AI Compresses Labor Income

1. Technological Divergence and the Structural Challenge to the Spanish Welfare State

Modern European welfare states were constructed on the foundational assumption of full-time, stable wage employment [@van-parijs-vanderborght-2017; @van-parijs-vanderborght-2017]. In Bismarckian social insurance systems such as Spain’s, entitlement to healthcare, unemployment protection, and retirement pensions is structurally linked to contributory payroll taxes (cotizaciones sociales) levied on formal labor earnings [@boe-ce-1978; @boe-trlgss-2015]. When technological change complements human cognitive capabilities, labor demand expands alongside productivity, preserving the wage bill and financing public redistribution [@acemoglu-restrepo-2019; @acemoglu-restrepo-2019].

The emergence of frontier generative artificial intelligence, agentic foundation models, and scalable autonomous software fundamentally breaks this historical complementarity [@eloundou-2024-gpts; @acemoglu-2024-macro-ai; @korinek-suh-2024]. Unlike 20th-century mechanization—which automated manual, repetitive physical tasks while shifting labor toward clerical, analytical, and managerial roles—AI directly substitutes for human cognitive, linguistic, and analytical labor across a wide spectrum of professions [@webb-2020-ai; @felten-2021-aioe; @gmyrek-ilo-2023]. In Spain, this shock interacts with deep-seated institutional vulnerabilities: 1. Persistent Structural Unemployment and Labor Duality: Spain’s unemployment rate has averaged 16.4% over the past four decades, consistently exceeding the Eurozone benchmark by 6 to 9 percentage points [@bentolila-2012-dual-spain]. Labor duality between highly protected indefinite contracts and precarious temporary or involuntary part-time work concentrates technological displacement risks among younger cohorts, women, and mid-tier service workers. 2. Fiscal Reliance on Payroll Levies: Social Security contributions account for approximately 34.5% of total tax revenue in Spain, representing 12.8% of GDP—significantly above the OECD average [@almunia-lopez-rodriguez-2019-eti]. A compression of the aggregate labor income share directly undermines the primary revenue engine of the Spanish welfare system. 3. The Operational Failure of Means-Tested Safety Nets: Introduced in 2020, Spain’s Ingreso Mínimo Vital (IMV [@boe-imv-2021]) was intended as a permanent non-contributory floor. However, official evaluations by Spain’s Independent Fiscal Authority (Autoridad Independiente de Responsabilidad Fiscal, AIReF [@airef-opinion-imv-2023; @airef-opinion-imv-2024]) document that 56% of eligible households living in severe poverty never access the benefit, while non-take-up reaches 70% for the child poverty supplement (Complemento de Ayuda para la Infancia, CAPI). Furthermore, ex-post means testing and retrospective income audits generate widespread administrative overpayment recovery orders (cobros indebidos), precipitating severe liquidity distress for vulnerable families.

Faced with structural labor income compression, piecemeal adjustments to means-tested schemes cannot preserve living standards. A fundamental redesign of social transfer architecture is required.

A. Universal Basic Rent (UBR / Renta Básica Universal)

Defined under Decision D-001 as an unconditional, periodic (monthly), individual cash transfer paid in euros to all qualifying residents without means testing or work requirements. While gross disbursements are strictly universal ex ante, UBR is integrated into the Personal Income Tax (Impuesto sobre la Renta de las Personas Físicas, IRPF [@boe-irpf-2006]) via an individual refundable tax credit (Deducción Reembolsable Universal). Consequently, upper-income earners contribute more in reformed taxes than they receive in gross transfers, making them net contributors ex post. Crucially, UBR treats the individual—not the cohabiting family—as the sovereign unit of welfare, eliminating bureaucratic scrutiny into cohabitation and safeguarding intra-household financial autonomy for women and dependent adults [@van-parijs-vanderborght-2017].

B. Guaranteed Minimum Income (Ingreso Mínimo Vital & Rentas Mínimas Autonómicas)

Targeted, means-tested cash assistance conditioned on prior-year declared income and wealth thresholds below statutory poverty ceilings [@boe-imv-2021]. By withdrawing €1 of benefit for every €1 of net earned income (or applying steep tapering schedules), minimum income schemes impose marginal effective tax rates (METRs) approaching 100%, generating acute poverty and inactivity traps. Furthermore, verifying cohabitation networks creates immense administrative friction, resulting in extensive non-take-up and retrospective debt notifications.

C. Negative Income Tax (NIT)

Originally formulated by Friedman [@ghatak-maniquet-2019], the NIT provides a guaranteed cash floor ($G$) that phases out at a constant marginal tax rate ($t$) as income ($Y$) increases: $\text{Transfer} = G - t \cdot Y$. While mathematically isomorphic to a UBR paired with a flat income tax in static annual budget terms [@saez-2002-optimal-transfers; @ghatak-maniquet-2019], the NIT differs markedly in real-world administrative delivery: 1. Disbursement Frequency & Liquidity: NIT is conventionally settled through annual income tax returns, leaving low-income households liquidity-constrained throughout the fiscal year. UBR provides liquid cash on the 1st of every month. 2. Intra-Household Allocation: NIT rebates are typically captured by the primary tax filer, obscuring intra-household inequality. Individualized UBR guarantees direct personal bank deposits to each adult. 3. Take-Up and Stigma: Universal enrollment bypasses the stigma and documentation costs inherent in income-tested tax credits, routinely lifting take-up above 98%.

Critical Methodological Limitations of Past Trials & The General-Equilibrium Financing Wedge

Despite their rich insights, existing micro-trials suffer from five foundational methodological boundaries that prevent naive direct extrapolation to nationwide policy: 1. The General-Equilibrium Financing Wedge: Microeconomic randomized trials (RCTs) evaluate temporary, unfinanced cash windfalls disbursed to an infinitesimal treatment subset within an undisturbed local economy [@chetty-2012-bounds]. Treatment recipients in pilots face an isolated unearned income effect while bearing zero liability for the macro-fiscal tax extraction required to finance the scheme; local consumer prices, aggregate labor supply, and market-clearing wages remain exogenous. In contrast, a nationwide, tax-financed UBR requires substantial tax extraction (progressive IRPF withholding, corporate surplus contributions), exposing workers simultaneously to an unearned income effect and an elevated marginal substitution effect from higher marginal effective tax rates (METRs). Consequently, micro-trial labor supply elasticities represent a structural lower bound on behavioral responses compared to a permanent general-equilibrium reform. 2. Absence of Spatial Saturation & SUTVA Failures: Dispersed individual randomization cannot capture local general-equilibrium wage, price, or rental pass-through spillovers [@baird-2018-interference]. If landlords in congested cities raise rents when tenants receive cash transfers, individual trials cannot detect the dissipation of benefit gains. 3. Finite-Horizon Horizon Bias: Three-year temporary grants conflate intertemporal labor substitution (taking a temporary Frisch “sabbatical” or enrolling in education) with permanent lifetime wealth effects [@vivalt-2024-openresearch; @bartik-2024-openresearch]. 4. Concave Notch Distortions: Experimental arms with clawbacks frequently impose abrupt phase-outs that generate concave kinks, obscuring structural substitution elasticities [@saez-2010-bunching; @kleven-2016-bunching]. 5. Administrative Attrition & Stigma Gaps: Uncompensated control groups suffer from 20–30% longitudinal survey attrition, while pilot delivery via dedicated research non-profits bypasses the bureaucratic friction, municipal gatekeeping, and digital divide barriers inherent in state administration.

4. Interaction with Spain’s Labor Market Institutions

The macroeconomic transmission of AI displacement is shaped by three institutional features of the Spanish labor framework [@boe-ce-1978; @boe-rdl-32-2021-reforma-laboral]: 1. Dismissal Regulation (Estatuto de los Trabajadores): Statutory severance pay for unfair dismissal (33 days per year worked, capped at 24 monthly payments) creates an initial friction that delays abrupt mass terminations in large enterprises. However, firms navigate this barrier by freezing entry-level hiring, outsourcing cognitive workflows to cloud AI APIs, and utilizing objective collective redundancy procedures (Despidos Colectivos, Art. 51 ET) grounded in technological and organizational causes. 2. Sectoral Collective Bargaining (Convenios Colectivos): Rigid sectoral wage floors prevent downward wage flexibility in formal unionized sectors [@bentolila-2012-dual-spain]. Consequently, when technological productivity expands outside the bargaining unit, labor adjustment occurs primarily along the employment margin rather than the wage margin, converting displacement into involuntary unemployment. 3. The Contributory Cliff in Unemployment Benefits: Contributory unemployment assistance (prestación contributiva por desempleo, managed by SEPE) provides 70% of the regulatory base for the first 180 days and 60% thereafter, terminating after a statutory ceiling of 24 months [@boe-trlgss-2015]. Displaced workers who exhaust contributory entitlement fall directly into the means-tested Subsidio por Desempleo (€480/month) or the non-take-up abyss of the IMV.

Sectoral Asymmetries: The Non-Automatable Low-Wage Service Dilemma

While AI primarily displaces cognitive, clerical, and analytical tasks (Groups 2–4), Spain’s labor market possesses massive manual, personal care, agricultural, and hospitality sectors (CNO-11 Groups 5, 8, and 9) with low technical automation exposure ($\text{Index} \le 0.25$). In these sectors, an uncalibrated transfer floor could induce supply-side friction: - Reservation Wage Spikes vs. Statutory Minimum Wage: With an adult Tier B transfer of €900/month (€1,800/month for a two-adult household), unearned income approaches the net statutory minimum wage (Salario Mínimo Interprofesional, SMI, approximately €1,134/month gross). In physically demanding, low-margin sectors that cannot substitute human labor with LLMs, reservation wages will rise, creating recruitment bottlenecks and cost-push wage inflation unless labor activation incentives are preserved [@chetty-2012-bounds]. - Human Capital Depreciation & Scarring Risks: Prolonged detachment from the formal labor market during structural technological shifts risks rapid human capital atrophy, professional network erosion, and permanent labor market scarring—particularly among younger cohorts at risk of swelling Spain’s NEET (Ni-Ni) population.

Institutional Counter-Weights: Smooth EITC-Style Taper & High-Dosage Training Fellowships

To eliminate labor supply withdrawal, avoid human capital atrophy, and prevent sharp marginal effective tax rate (METR) bunching cliffs, UBR is paired with two structural labor-market instruments: 1. The Earned-Income Formalization Bonus (Bonificación de Integración Laboral Formal): Rather than a hard exemption cliff that induces hours rationing at €6,000, the benefit integrates a continuous, Spanish EITC-style phase-out schedule: - Exempt Tranche: The first €6,000 of formal wage income is completely exempt from clawback withholdings (0% clawback rate). - Smooth Taper Schedule: Between €6,000 and €18,000 of gross annual earnings, the bonus phases out smoothly at a moderate 20% taper rate. Combined with employee social security contributions (6.35%) and baseline regional-state IRPF rates, total combined METR remains strictly bounded below 51.8% across the entire distribution, preventing the acute >65% notch cliffs and polynomial bunching mass documented under legacy social assistance [@saez-2010; @kleven-2016]. Every additional hour worked yields a guaranteed, positive net disposable income return. 2. High-Dosage Technological Fellowships (Cheque Formación Tecnológica Avanzado): Disbursing an annual €1,200 training entitlement that can be accumulated or front-loaded across 3 years (€3,600) for immersive STEM, applied engineering, and algorithmic technical bootcamps: - Value-Added Provider Accreditation: Conditioning provider accreditation on verified graduate employment rates and TGSS wage uplifts, strictly precluding low-value diploma mills or FUNDAE-style subsidy fraud. - Dual Corporate Apprenticeships: Vouchers are co-matched with accredited private technology and industrial employers, guaranteeing direct hands-on apprenticeships and SEPE ledger anti-fraud verification. This transforms UBR from a passive leisure subsidy into an active platform for continuous mid-career human capital retooling.

2. The No-Loser Constraint: Harmonizing IMV, Minimum Incomes, and Disability

A fatal flaw in naive basic income models is the mechanical abolition of existing targeted assistance, which inadvertently harms large families or disabled individuals with high legacy entitlements [@hoynes-rothstein-2019]. To satisfy the Pareto-improvement criterion, the reform institutes an enforceable No-Loser Guarantee (Garantía de Indemnidad de Derechos Adquiridos):

$$\text{Transfer}_i = \max\left(\text{UBR}_i, \, \text{LegacyEntitlement}_i\right)$$

A. Folding the Ingreso Mínimo Vital (IMV) and Resolving the “IMV Paradox”

  • All non-contributory cash transfers under Ley 19/2021 (IMV [@boe-imv-2021]) and autonomous community minimum insertion incomes (Rentas Mínimas de Inserción) are integrated into the baseline UBR structure.
  • Overcoming the “IMV Paradox” in Transition Top-Ups: Under legacy IMV, 56% of eligible households in severe poverty never receive benefits due to intrusive cohabitation audits, and recipients suffer from retrospective overpayment debt notices (cobros indebidos). To prevent the transition top-up from reproducing this exact pathology, the Complemento Personal Transitorio de Mantenimiento de Ingresos (€7.50 billion per year in Tier B) is legally individualized:
  • De Jure Individualization: The differential supplement is permanently allocated to the primary caregiver registered on the baseline certification date, without requiring ongoing cohabitation unit audits or annual household asset recertification.
  • Statutory Immunity from Retrospective Clawbacks: Any minor administrative overpayment arising from automated data latency is statutorily treated as a non-recoverable social transition credit, strictly prohibiting administrative asset seizure or sudden payment suspension unless deliberate criminal fraud (Art. 307 ter Código Penal) is established by a final court order.
  • Household Integrity & Anti-Splitting Safeguards: To prevent tactical domestic restructuring (fictitious single-parent declarations or artificial cohabitation dissolutions designed to multiply transition supplements), the statute establishes automated cadastral cohabitation checks cross-referencing AEAT IRPF joint tax returns over the preceding three fiscal years. Allocation of child supplements in contested custody situations requires a ratified judicial agreement (convenio regulador judicialmente aprobado), and single-parent status changes are subject to a 24-month audit look-back.

B. Tiered Universal Disability Protection

Disability entitlements are decoupled from income testing and integrated via an impairment-scaled universal supplement (Complemento Universal por Discapacidad) indexed to official dependency assessments under Real Decreto 888/2022 [@boe-rd-888-2022-discapacidad]: - Degree 1 (Moderate, 33–64%): +25% of baseline UBR (+€225/mo in Tier B). - Degree 2 (Severe, 65–74%): +50% of baseline UBR (+€450/mo). - Degree 3 (Great Dependency, $\ge 75\%$): +100% of baseline UBR (+€900/mo). This architecture reduces impairment-adjusted poverty from 24.4% to 6.28%, dismantling the institutional poverty trap where disabled persons lose benefits upon taking up part-time employment.

Anteproyecto de Ley de Renta Básica Universal

Texto legal completo estructurado en 9 Títulos (25 artículos), Disposiciones Adicionales, Transitorias, Derogatoria y Finales. Incorpora la Exposición de Motivos, el Fondo de Compensación CPFF, el anclaje constitucional (Arts. 149.1.1, 149.1.14 y 149.1.17 CE), el Impuesto IECA y el Parámetro Fiscal Federal de Vivienda.

⚖️ Exposición de Motivos (Haga clic para desplegar los antecedentes y fundamentos doctrinales)
I. Marco de Transformación Tecnológica y Necesidad Normativa.

La economía española y el conjunto de la Unión Europea afrontan una transformación estructural sin precedentes derivada de la rápida difusión de sistemas de inteligencia artificial generativa, modelos fundacionales de aprendizaje profundo y automatización avanzada. La evidencia empírica confirma que dicha disrupción incide de forma asimétrica sobre el mercado laboral, comprimiendo la participación del factor trabajo en la renta nacional y polarizando el empleo en ocupaciones tanto cognitivas como de servicios. Frente a este escenario, los mecanismos tradicionales de garantía de ingresos condicionados, caracterizados por la fragmentación administrativa, la comprobación retrospectiva de recursos y elevadas tasas de no cobertura involuntaria, resultan insuficientes para sostener la cohesión social.

II. Fundamento Constitucional y Naturaleza Jurídica.

La presente Ley instaura la Renta Básica Universal (RBU) como un derecho subjetivo, individual, universal e incondicional de toda persona residente en territorio español. Su arquitectura descansa de modo primordial en las competencias exclusivas del Estado sobre regulación de las condiciones básicas que garanticen la igualdad de todos los españoles en el ejercicio de los derechos y en el cumplimiento de los deberes constitucionales (artículo 149.1.1.ª de la Constitución Española), Hacienda general y Deuda del Estado (artículo 149.1.14.ª CE), y legislación básica y régimen económico de la Seguridad Social (artículo 149.1.17.ª CE), en directa concordancia con el mandato de suficiencia protectora consagrado en el artículo 41 CE y la doctrina del Tribunal Constitucional expresada en las Sentencias 158/2021 y 19/2024.

III. Articulación Tributaria en el IRPF y Simetría Fiscal.

El instrumento central de instrumentación es la Deducción Reembolsable Universal por Renta Básica integrada en la Ley 35/2006 del Impuesto sobre la Renta de las Personas Físicas. A través de este mecanismo, liquidado mensualmente de forma anticipada y automática, se garantiza que cada persona adulta perciba la cuantía legalmente fijada sin incurrir en trampas de pobreza ni desincentivos al empleo. Se preserva rigurosamente el mínimo de subsistencia (STC 214/1994) para aquellos contribuyentes en período transitorio de carencia, y se establece un Parámetro Fiscal Federal Objetivo en los arrendamientos de vivienda para neutralizar la captura de rentas sin interferir en competencias autonómicas.

IV. Captura de Rentas Algorítmicas y Sostenibilidad Financiera.

Para responder al desplazamiento del factor trabajo, se articula el Impuesto Especial sobre Capacidad y Computación de Inteligencia Artificial (IECA) en el Texto Refundido de la Ley del Impuesto sobre la Renta de no Residentes, cerrando la vía de elusión mediante la condicionalidad de deducibilidad en el Impuesto sobre Sociedades (artículo 15 LIS). Asimismo, se crea el Fondo Soberano de Transición Algorítmica (FSTA) bajo el artículo 128.2 CE, dotado de un blindaje parlamentario reforzado y reglas de acumulación anticíclicas conformes con el Reglamento (UE) 2024/1263.

V. Eficiencia Administrativa, Privacidad y Cohesión Territorial.

La gestión se confía a la Cuenta Única Tributaria-Social operada coordinadamente por la AEAT y el INSS, salvaguardando la privacidad laboral mediante un Código de Retención Sintético y estableciendo blindajes absolutos frente a embargos indebidos. En el ámbito territorial, se aprueba el Fondo de Compensación UBR en el marco del Consejo de Política Fiscal y Financiera para el régimen común y se salvaguarda la naturaleza foral del Concierto y Convenio mediante la calificación como carga no asumida.

VI. Gobernanza Dinámica y Diálogo Social.

Finalmente, la Ley prevé circuitos de alarma gobernados por la certificación técnica independiente de la AIReF, salvaguardando el principio de legalidad tributaria (STC 73/2017) y blindando la negociación colectiva mediante cláusulas de no suplantación salarial acordadas con los interlocutores sociales.
