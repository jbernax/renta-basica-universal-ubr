# Review Synthesis — Iteration 0002 (`iter-0002`)

## Overview & Panel Consensus
The five specialist reviewers (`social-policy`, `evaluation-methodologist`, `housing-economist`, `political-economist`, and `red-team`) completed comprehensive audits of the Iteration 1 research plan. The panel recognized the major structural improvements achieved in `iter-0001`—particularly the Status-Quo Budget Neutrality framework, Dual-Pillar pension integration, and open-economy HANK closure—while identifying critical institutional, empirical, and econometric vulnerabilities that must be resolved in Iteration 2:

1. **Macro-Micro Fiscal Coherence:** A quantitative chasm exists between M01's macroeconomic projection of an 11.55% of GDP baseline tax revenue collapse under S2 and M03's static assumption that the status-quo public service baseline remains intact from the existing wage base.
2. **Experimental Identification & Spatial Interference:** M10's cluster saturation design suffers from severe spatial interference (SUTVA violations) when using narrow urban census tracts without buffer zones, while the randomized clawback arm phases out to zero above median earnings, failing to provide experimental variation for middle/upper deciles.
3. **Housing Market & Regulatory Realities:** Landlord rent capture cannot be modeled as a frictionless uniform market without accounting for LAU Art. 9 5-year tenure lock-in for incumbent tenants, contract-substitution loopholes (seasonal and room leases bypassing Ley 12/2023 price caps), closed-loop landlord rental income recycling in EUROMOD, and immediate fiscal/regulatory circuit-breakers instead of 4–7 year public housing construction lags.
4. **Constitutional Legality & Bicameral Governance:** Delegating automatic tax surcharges and benefit tier triggers to AIReF violates the constitutional Principle of Tax Legality (Arts. 31.3 & 134.7 CE) and exceeds its statutory evaluative mandate under LO 6/2013; legislative enactment must incorporate Spain's bicameral veto architecture (Senado absolute veto under LOEPSF Art. 15), social dialogue (Art. 7 CE), and regionalist swing caucuses.
5. **Welfare Architecture & Single-Parent/Disability Protection:** Regional minimum incomes (RGI/RGC) cannot be wiped as a central dividend without violating the No-Loser Guarantee in high-benefit regions (Euskadi/Navarre); single mothers face benefit cliffs under Tier A without an explicit single-parent supplement; and non-contributory disability pensions cannot be flatly absorbed without an extra-costs-of-disability supplement.
6. **Citation-Claim Hygiene:** Anachronistic citations (e.g. citing a 2019 Basque study for post-2021 IMV non-take-up) must be excised and replaced with official AIReF evaluation reports and verified legal/empirical benchmarks.

All candidate changes have been synthesized into 9 high-leverage proposals (`SYN-02-01` to `SYN-02-09`). None violate human-locked decisions in `plan/decisions.md`.

## Synthesized Proposals

### `SYN-02-01` · Macro-Micro Fiscal Loop & S2/S3 Baseline Tax Erosion
- **Contributing Issues:** `RT-02-01` (critical), `RT-02-04` (major)
- **Target Files:** `plan/methods.md`, `plan/modules/M01-ai-labor-displacement.md`, `plan/modules/M03-fiscal-financing.md`, `plan/modules/M16-ai-rent-capture-sovereign-fund.md`
- **Actions:**
  1. In `methods.md` and `M03`, close the feedback loop between macro displacement and micro tax simulation: mandate that EUROMOD-Spain runs directly on counterfactual post-displacement wage and employment distributions generated by M01/HANK across S1, S2, and S3, rather than unadjusted static survey weights.
  2. In `M03`, explicitly incorporate the 11.55% of GDP baseline tax revenue collapse under S2 into the consolidated Administraciones Públicas fiscal bridge, specifying that baseline public services (~€125B/year) must be co-financed by expanding corporate surplus taxes (`M16`), consumption taxes, and wealth levies as the labor tax base contracts.
  3. In `M16`, clarify that the Sovereign AI Dividend Fund is an intergenerational capital buffer yielding 0.5–0.8% of GDP/year, which partially buffers transition costs but cannot fund the entirety of Tier C, requiring explicit specification of the current tax bases supporting higher tiers.
- **New Sources:** `acemoglu-2024-macro-ai`, `korinek-suh-2024`, `hoynes-rothstein-2019`.

### `SYN-02-02` · Spatial Saturation Buffers, SUTVA, and Clawback Kink Econometrics
- **Contributing Issues:** `EM-02-01` (critical), `EM-02-02` (critical), `RT-02-02` (critical)
- **Target Files:** `plan/modules/M10-pilot-evaluation-design.md`, `plan/methods.md`
- **Actions:**
  1. Redefine cluster randomization units in `M10` and `methods.md` from narrow, porous urban census tracts to economically self-contained spatial units: distinct small municipalities (1,500–5,000 residents) in non-metropolitan areas and transit-bounded districts in urban zones, enforcing mandatory geographic buffer zones ($\ge 2\text{--}5$ km in rural/peri-urban settings; donut matching in urban areas) per Baird et al. (2018) to prevent SUTVA spatial contamination.
  2. Formulate the explicit cluster MDE formula with empirical Spanish parameters ($\rho_{\text{rent}} = 0.08$, $\rho_{\text{labor}} = 0.03$, cluster size $m \approx 285$, $R^2 \approx 0.35$ from pre-trial INCASÒL/MCVL microdata).
  3. Address the clawback threshold kink ($Y^* = \text{UBR} / \tau_{\text{clawback}}$): stratify experimental samples into baseline earnings domains and integrate a pre-registered bunching estimator (Saez 2010; Chetty 2012) around $Y^*$ to estimate compensated substitution elasticities directly from excess mass, explicitly acknowledging that upper-decile tax responses are estimated quasi-experimentally via administrative IRPF panel reforms (`M08`).
- **New Sources:** `baird-2018-interference` (DOI: 10.1162/rest_a_00716), `duflo-2007-randomization` (DOI: 10.1016/S1573-4471(07)04061-2), `saez-2010-bunching` (DOI: 10.1257/pol.2.3.180), `chetty-2012-bounds` (DOI: 10.3982/ECTA9043).

### `SYN-02-04` · Housing Law, LAU Tenure Lock-In, Seasonal Leases & Closed-Loop Rent Recycling
- **Contributing Issues:** `HE-02-01` (critical), `HE-02-03` (major), `HE-02-04` (critical), `RT-02-06` (minor)
- **Target Files:** `plan/modules/M05-housing-rent-capture.md`, `plan/modules/M09-distribution-poverty-euromod.md`, `plan/modules/M17-transition-risks-stress-tests.md`
- **Actions:**
  1. In `M05`, formulate a dual-tenure equilibrium framework modeling LAU Art. 9 mandatory 5-year lease stability for incumbent tenants vs. new contracts, and address contract-substitution loopholes (landlords shifting to seasonal leases under LAU Art. 3.2 or room rentals under CC Art. 1554 to bypass Ley 12/2023 caps). Condition LIRPF Art. 23.2 deductions on long-term habitual residence contracts adhering to reference price limits.
  2. In `M09`, disaggregate households across 4 distinct housing tenure regimes (outright owners, mortgaged owners, subsidized renters, market renters) and implement closed-loop rent recycling: aggregate landlord rent extraction is credited to landlord capital income in AEAT microdata and taxed under reformed IRPF (`M03`). Report housing-cost overburden rates (>40% of disposable income) alongside headline poverty.
  3. In `M17`, formally integrate the Housing Pass-Through Circuit-Breaker promised by `M05` (activating if quarterly rental index growth exceeds core CPI by >3.0 pp in stressed zones ZMRT). Replace slow 4–7 year public housing construction triggers with immediate fiscal/regulatory levers: dynamic revocation of LIRPF Art. 23.2 landlord deductions (50% to 0%), emergency extension of price caps to seasonal/room rentals, and municipal fast-track *tanteo y retracto* housing acquisitions.
- **New Sources:** `garcia-montalvo-2023-rent-controls` (DOI: 10.24148/wp2023-28), `jofre-monseny-2023-rent-control` (DOI: 10.1016/j.regsciurbeco.2023.103916), `boe-lau-1994` (URL: https://www.boe.es/eli/es/l/1994/11/24/29).

### `SYN-02-05` · Constitutional Tax Legality of Triggers & Bicameral Veto-Player Architecture
- **Contributing Issues:** `PE-02-01` (critical), `PE-02-02` (critical), `PE-02-03` (major)
- **Target Files:** `plan/modules/M11-political-economy.md`, `plan/modules/M17-transition-risks-stress-tests.md`
- **Actions:**
  1. In `M17`, realign transition triggers with the constitutional Principle of Tax Legality (Arts. 31.3 and 134.7 CE) and AIReF's statutory organic mandate under Ley Orgánica 6/2013. Reposition AIReF strictly as an independent technical auditor issuing non-binding Technological Transition Reports ('comply or explain'). Replace automatic administrative tax surcharges with a statutory Fast-Track Parliamentary Ratification Protocol under Art. 86 CE / Reglamento del Congreso mandating government decree-laws within 30 days of trigger certification.
  2. In `M11`, embed a formal bicameral veto-player framework (Tsebelis 2002) modeling spatial equilibria across both the Congreso and the Senado (specifically addressing the Senate's absolute veto over expenditure ceilings under LOEPSF Art. 15). Detail concessions required for pro-business regionalist swing caucuses (PNV, Junts: decentralized tax tranches, SME surplus exemptions, transition fund co-management).
  3. In `M11`, formalize tripartite social dialogue under Art. 7 CE and the CES (Ley 21/1991), establishing statutory non-supplantation clauses (UBR cannot be cited by employers to depress bargained wages or SMI hikes) and social partner co-governance of automation funds.
- **New Sources:** `tsebelis-2002-veto-players` (DOI: 10.1515/9781400831456), `boe-lo-6-2013-airef` (URL: https://www.boe.es/eli/es/lo/2013/11/14/6), `vanderborght-2006-trade-unions` (DOI: 10.2202/1932-0183.1002), `vlandas-2019-politics-ubi` (DOI: 10.1515/bis-2018-0021).

### `SYN-02-06` · Asymmetric Fiscal Federalism, Regional Minimum Incomes & Single-Parent Protection
- **Contributing Issues:** `SP-02-01` (critical), `PE-02-04` (major)
- **Target Files:** `plan/modules/M02-benefit-design.md`, `plan/modules/M06-constitutional-eu-law.md`, `plan/modules/M07-welfare-benefit-integration.md`
- **Actions:**
  1. In `M07`, eliminate the erroneous assumption that regional minimum incomes (RGI/RGC) are abolished as a central dividend. Instead, codify a statutory *Complemento Autonómico No Contributivo* preserving regional entitlement rights and housing supplements (PEV/PCV in Euskadi/Navarre) that exceed the national UBR floor, holding all regional recipients strictly harmless.
  2. In `M02` and `M07`, introduce an explicit *Complemento Monoparental y de Primera Infancia* so that single-parent families (81% female-headed) receiving the 22% IMV bonus and CAPI never suffer a disposable income loss under Tier A (€600 adult + €240 child vs. €965.55 IMV+CAPI baseline).
  3. In `M06` and `M11`, specify the bilateral methodology for the Basque *Cupo* (6.24%) and Navarrese *Aportación* (1.6%) by classifying national UBR as a non-assumed State power (*carga no asumida*), neutralizing territorial fiscal arbitrage, and establish CPFF mechanisms to hold net-contributing common-regime regions harmless in public services.
- **New Sources:** `beramendi-2012-inequality` (DOI: 10.1017/CBO9781139042796), `airef-opinion-imv-2023` / `airef-opinion-imv-2024`.

- **Actions:**
  1.
