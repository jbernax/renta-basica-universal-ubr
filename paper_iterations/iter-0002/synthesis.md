## 2. Steelmanned Synthesis & Actionable Proposals

### SYN-02-01: Fiscal Anti-Avoidance & Pillar Two Harmonization
- **Issues Addressed:** `ADV-FISCAL-02-01`, `ADV-FISCAL-02-02`, `ADV-FISCAL-02-04`.
- **Target Chapters:** `paper/ch04-fiscal-architecture-ai-rent-capture.md`.
- **Action:**
  1. *IECA Auditing & Telemetry:* Introduce a statutory look-through presumption (*presunción de destino compute*) in Title III: bundled SaaS/digital services consumed by Spanish IPs/domains default to a 25% compute imputation ratio unless disproved by audited telemetry (expanded Model 238/DAC7). Establish joint-and-several withholding liability for tier-1 telecom interconnect carriers and colocation providers.
  2. *OECD Pillar Two Covered Tax Qualification:* Statutorily define the €36.50B EBE contribution as an excess operating profit levy on net accounting margin, qualifying as a Covered Tax under Article 20 of Directive 2022/2523 (GloBE ETR numerator). Harmonize with the Substance-Based Income Exclusion (SBIE) phase-down and introduce an Undertaxed Profits Rule (UTPR) anti-base-erosion mechanism.
  3. *FSTA Counter-Cyclical Solvent Architecture:* Model a 5-year stochastic equity drawdown (-35%). Adopt a university-endowment-style Constant-Endowment Smoothing Rule (5-year moving average cap on payouts) and establish a segregated Tier-1 Liquidity Reserve (24 months of payouts in short-term euro T-bills) to decouple from market drawdowns and sovereign bond spread spikes.

### SYN-02-02: Macroeconomic Input-Output Closure & Scenario S3 NPE Path
- **Issues Addressed:** `ADV-FISCAL-02-03`, `ADV-PROD-02-03`.
- **Target Chapters:** `paper/ch05-macroeconomic-closure-housing-eurozone.md`.
- **Action:**
  1. *Scenario S3 NPE Trajectory:* Provide explicit multi-year NPE trajectory under Tier C and -15.0 pp labor share compression. Formalize legal invocation of the Article 25 Severe Economic Downturn Clause under Regulation (EU) 2024/1263, calibrating the Article 18 Control Account with an asymmetric consumption stabilizer.
  2. *Input-Output Multi-Sector Tradable Closure:* Expand the open-economy model with INE/WIOD input-output cost-push pass-through for Spain's top export engines: Automotive & Components (C29), Industrial Machinery (C28), and Agrifood (C10-C11). Demonstrate how the 4 pp employer social contribution reduction and targeted energy/logistics credits shield tradable margins against intermediate service wage pressures.

### SYN-02-03: Labor Supply Continuity & Care Economy Safeguards
- **Issues Addressed:** `ADV-PROD-02-01`, `ADV-PROD-02-02`, `ADV-PROD-02-04`.
- **Target Chapters:** `paper/ch02-ai-shock-spain.md`, `paper/ch03-benefit-architecture-welfare-integration.md`.
- **Action:**
  1. *Smooth METR Taper:* Replace the hard €6,000 exemption cliff with a smooth, continuous earned-income taper (integrated Spanish EITC schedule tapering at 20-25% between €6,000 and €18,000), bounding total effective METRs below 52% and eliminating bunching cliffs.
  2. *High-Dosage Technological Fellowship:* Redesign the *Cheque Formación Tecnológica* into a performance-contingent fellowship linked to TGSS employment uplift, allowing multi-year capital pooling (€3,600 over 3 years) for immersive STEM bootcamps with dual-training corporate apprenticeships and SEPE ledger anti-fraud verification.
  3. *Care Sector Wage Supplement:* Introduce the *Complemento Sectorial de Cuidados y Dependencia* maintaining a statutory 35% wage premium over the UBR floor for institutional eldercare and nursery workers, preventing female workforce withdrawal.

### SYN-02-04: Housing Windfall Tax Administration & Macroprudential Tiering
- **Issues Addressed:** `ADV-HOUSE-02-01`, `ADV-HOUSE-02-02`, `ADV-HOUSE-02-03`, `ADV-HOUSE-02-04`.
- **Target Chapters:** `paper/ch05-macroeconomic-closure-housing-eurozone.md`, `paper/ch08-political-economy-transition-governance.md`.
- **Action:**
  1. *Tiered Macroprudential DSTI Rule:* Replace the rigid 35% nominal transfer haircut with a tiered framework: allow 70% UBR recognition for first-time primary residence buyers under MIVAU price caps backed by an ICO mortgage guarantee, while imposing 0% UBR recognition and a 40% equity down-payment requirement (60% LTV) on buy-to-let/investor mortgages. Include banking sector CET1 stress analysis.
  2. *AEAT-Administered Windfall Tax with Improvement Deductions:* Transfer primary administration of the 90% *Gravamen Extraordinario* to AEAT via Electronic Billing/TicketBAI cross-checks with the Cadastre. Codify a *Régimen de Amortización de Mejoras Acreditadas* allowing an 8% annual depreciation deduction for verified energy efficiency and structural renovations.
  3. *Co-Living & Flex-Living Anti-Circumvention:* Enact an *All-Inclusive Consideration Rule* (*Principio de Retribución Íntegra*) classifying all mandatory ancillary fees as rent under MIVAU caps. Establish an irrebuttable economic reality presumption (>60 cumulative days in 12 months deemed residential tenancy), with joint and several liability and automated reporting for digital rental platforms.
  4. *Intermediate Secondary Cities Archetype:* Model *Ciudades Intermedias* (pop 25,000–100,000) in Chapter 5 and Chapter 8, allocating 15 saturation pilot clusters to intermediate towns and extending the Federal Fiscal Benchmark to intermediate urban corridors.

### SYN-02-05: Administrative Delivery, AML/KYC, & Constitutional Impasse
- **Issues Addressed:** `ADV-IMPL-02-01`, `ADV-IMPL-02-02`, `ADV-IMPL-02-03`, `ADV-IMPL-02-04`, `ADV-IMPL-02-05`, `ADV-LAW-02-01`, `ADV-LAW-02-02`, `ADV-LAW-02-03`, `ADV-LAW-02-04`, `ADV-LAW-02-05`.
- **Target Chapters:** `paper/ch06-constitutional-territorial-eu-legal-blueprint.md`, `paper/ch08-political-economy-transition-governance.md`.
- **Action:**
  1. *Rural Liquidity & Simplified Due Diligence:* Deploy the *Correos Rural* mobile network with subsidized liquidity transport under the Universal Postal Service Obligation (SPU). Enact a Simplified Due Diligence Tier under RD 304/2014 Art. 9 and AMLD5 Art. 12, allowing card issuance on municipal padrón with social report, backed by merchant POS and zero-commission retail cash-back.
  2. *Inter-Administrative Data Protocols & PID:* Mandate the *Servicio Federado de Liquidación UBR* under the State Data Intermediation Platform (PID, Ley 40/2015 Art. 155) with standardized Open-API endpoints and event-driven TGSS/AEAT settlement tokens.
  3. *Safeguarded Dispute Resolution & Foral Impasse:* Delineate positive silence under Art. 24.1 LPACAP by establishing an *Anticipo Provisional Cautelar Condicionado* (retaining negative silence formally to prevent fraud consolidation). Codify an explicit Foral Impasse Resolution Protocol under Ley 12/2002 Art. 64 with a joint arbitration tribunal under Supreme Court chairmanship.
  4. *Multilingual Parity & GDPR Telemetry Ring-Fence:* Mandate native administrative processing across Castilian, Catalan/Valencian, Galician, and Basque under Art. 15 LPACAP and Art. 3/139 CE. Restrict utility/telemetry queries strictly to secondary targeted administrative fraud audits under judicial oversight, adhering to GDPR Art. 5(1)(c) and STC 76/2019.
  5. *Tripartite EU & Third-Country Framework:* Categorize entitlements into Spanish/Long-Term TCNs (Directive 2003/109/EC), EU Workers (Art. 45 TFEU), and Inactive EU/Temporary TCNs (Directive 2004/38/EC with Brey proportionality reviews).
  6. *Foral Corporate Anti-Circumvention & Decree-Law Fallbacks:* Extend the 5-year look-back to closely held corporations (*sociedades patrimoniales*). In Chapter 8, formalize the constitutional boundary of Art. 86 CE under SSTC 73/2017 & 78/2020, articulating LPGE budget bill contingencies if Organic Law 15.6 is delayed.
