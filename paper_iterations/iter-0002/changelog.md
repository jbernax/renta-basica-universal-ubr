- **Issues Addressed:** `ADV-PROD-02-01`, `ADV-PROD-02-02`, `ADV-PROD-02-04`.

  - Modeled Intermediate Secondary Cities (*ciudades intermedias*, pop 25k–100k) and allocated 16 saturation pilot clusters to provincial hubs.
