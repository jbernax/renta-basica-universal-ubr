# Cross-Cutting Methodological Architecture (`methods.md`)

This document specifies the five core analytical pillars linking the 17 modules (`M01`–`M17`) into a closed, internally consistent research pipeline.

## 1. Task-Based AI Exposure, Spanish Labor Institutions, & Scenario Projections (`M01`, `M08`, `M16`)
- **Occupational Mapping & Task Weights:** Crosswalk US O*NET task exposure indices (Eloundou et al. $\beta$-exposure [@eloundou-2024-gpts], Felten AIOE [@felten-2021-aioe], Webb patent-task overlap [@webb-2020-ai], and ILO Gen-AI scores [@gmyrek-ilo-2023]) to ISCO-08 and Spain's 3-digit *Clasificación Nacional de Ocupaciones* (CNO-11) using INE *Encuesta de Población Activa* (EPA) microdata and European Skills/Competences (ESCO) task weights [@albanesi-2023-ai-europe].
- **Spanish Institutional Adjustment Channels:** Model how sector-level collective bargaining (*convenios colectivos* with *ultraactividad* under *Real Decreto-ley 32/2021* [@boe-rdl-32-2021-reforma-laboral]), statutory collective bargaining opt-outs (*descuelgues salariales* under Art. 82.3 ET and collective dismissals under Art. 51 ET [@bentolila-2020-dual-labor]), statutory gross SMI labor cost floors ($W_{\text{floor}} \approx \text{€}1,740/\text{month}$ per *RD 145/2024* [@rd-145-2024-smi; @barcelo-2021-smi]), and firm-size adoption lags (from INE ETICCE) channel AI substitution into extensive-margin displacement cliffs and intermittent contract inactivity (*pases a inactividad de fijos discontinuos* in MCVL) alongside ERTE/RED mechanisms [@bentolila-2012-dual-spain].
- **Sequential Decomposition Protocol (Avoiding Double-Counting):**
  1. *Step 1 (Demand-Side Involuntary Displacement):* Calibrate an Acemoglu-Restrepo task-allocation framework [@acemoglu-restrepo-2019; @acemoglu-2024-macro-ai] to Spanish Contabilidad Nacional and MCVL longitudinal records via microdata separation hazard models, projecting involuntary displacement hazards, wage-bill compression, and employer Social Security payroll revenue erosion across **S1** ($\Delta s_L = -2.5$ pp, $\Delta \text{Rev}_{\text{base}} \approx -0.98\%$ GDP), **S2** ($-7.0$ pp, $\Delta \text{Rev}_{\text{base}} \approx -2.73\%$ GDP), and **S3** ($-15.0$ pp, $\Delta \text{Rev}_{\text{base}} \approx -5.85\%$ GDP) [@korinek-suh-2024; @cazzaniga-imf-2024]. Pre-trend balance and shift-share IV orthogonality are tested per Borusyak, Hull, & Jaravel [@borusyak-2022-shift-share].
  2. *Step 2 (Supply-Side Behavioral Response):* Apply structural discrete-choice and collective labor-supply elasticities (`M08`, `M14`) to the post-displacement counterfactual market income distribution.

## 2. Static & Behavioral Tax-Benefit Microsimulation (`M02`, `M03`, `M07`, `M09`, `M14`)
- **EUROMOD-Spain Backbone & Counterfactual Post-Displacement Loop:** Implement UBR Tiers A (€600/€240), B (€900/€360), and C (€1,200/€480) inside EUROMOD-Spain [@sutherland-figari-2013-euromod] running on the *Panel de Hogares* (IEF/AEAT/INE) and ECV, supplemented by the *Muestra de Declarantes de IRPF* for top-tail Pareto calibration. Crucially, EUROMOD-Spain runs directly on counterfactual post-displacement wage and employment distributions generated dynamically by M01/HANK across S1, S2, and S3, rather than static historical survey weights.
- **Status-Quo Budget Neutrality & Consolidated AP Fiscal Bridge:** Total post-reform revenues must preserve 100% of baseline public services (~€125B/year) PLUS gross UBR outlays minus abolished non-contributory cash transfers (`M07`). Under Scenario S2, the $-7.0$ pp labor share contraction induces an 11.55% relative compression in labor tax receipts, generating an empirical baseline payroll and IRPF revenue erosion of $2.73\%$ of GDP (~€41.0B/year on Spain's €1,500B GDP). The consolidated public accounts bridge explicitly covers this gap by combining Destination-Based AI Compute and Inference Withholding excises (*IECA*, `M16`, `M03`), corporate automation surplus taxes, consumption tax broadening, and net wealth levies. Contributory pensions above the UBR floor (~5.5–6.0% of GDP) are preserved under a Dual-Pillar structure with an explicit *Complemento de Neutralidad Fiscal* ensuring zero disposable-income loss for any pensioner or vulnerable recipient (`M07`), alongside a statutory *Garantía de Mantenimiento de Bases de Cotización por Desempleo* (Art. 273 TRLGSS). LIRPF Art. 20 and Art. 84 are folded into the unified schedule [@boe-irpf-2006].
- **Indirect Tax & Welfare Evaluation:** Estimate a Quadratic Almost Ideal Demand System (QUAIDS) on EPF to model behavioral consumption responses to IVA base broadening [@boe-iva-1992], evaluating welfare impacts via Compensating/Equivalent Variation ($EV/CV$) per Banks, Blundell & Lewbel [@banks-1997-quaids] and household-specific COICOP price indices to evaluate real Before-Housing-Cost (BHC) and After-Housing-Cost (AHC) poverty (FGT $P_0, P_1, P_2$) and inequality (Gini/Atkinson).

## 3. Open-Economy Currency-Union HANK General-Equilibrium Closure (`M03`, `M04`, `M05`, `M16`, `M17`)
- **Monetary Union HANK Specification:** Calibrate a two-sector (tradable vs. non-tradable/housing) open-economy HANK model for Spain [@kaplan-moll-violante-2018-hank; @auclert-rognlie-straub-2024; @gali-monacelli-2005-soe] with area-wide ECB Taylor-rule response (Spain weight ~10%), Walters real-interest-rate dynamics, terms-of-trade competitiveness channels, and endogenous Bonos-Bund sovereign spread feedback.
- **Dual External Leakage & Fiscal Rules:** Account for Balance of Payments leakage from both household tradable consumption imports (by decile) and intermediate business imports of foreign AI compute and model APIs. Embed the reformed EU fiscal governance framework (*Regulation (EU) 2024/1263* [@eu-reg-2024-1263-fiscal-framework; @regulation-eu-2024-1263-net-expenditure]) and Spain's *Ley Orgánica 2/2012* (LOEPSF [@boe-loepsf-2012]), verifying the multi-annual Net Primary Expenditure trajectory, Control Account debit ceilings (0.3%/0.6% GDP), and ECB Transmission Protection Instrument (TPI) eligibility.
- **Four-Stage Iterative Micro-Macro Closure Pipeline:**
  1. *Handoff 1 (Macro to Micro):* `M01` scenario vectors project occupational displacement and market wage-bill compression onto micro-records.
  2. *Handoff 2 (Micro Equilibrium):* EUROMOD iterates with `M08` labor supply and `M03` ETI parameters [@almunia-lopez-rodriguez-2019-eti] to solve for the consolidated revenue-neutral tax schedule.
  3. *Handoff 3 (Micro to Macro):* Decile net transfers, effective marginal tax rates, and labor-supply shifts feed into HANK (`M04`) and the spatial housing model (`M05`) to solve for equilibrium wages, non-tradable CPI, urban rental pass-through, and real GDP.
  4. *Handoff 4 (Macro Feedback Loop):* Dynamic general-government closure incorporates sovereign debt service shifts ($\Delta \text{Overall Balance}_t = \Delta \text{Primary Balance}_t - \Delta ((r_t - g_t) D_{t-1}) - \Delta S_t^{\text{Fund}} = 0$) per Blanchard [@blanchard-2019-public-debt], iterating via a Gauss-Seidel fixed-point algorithm until the structural primary balance converges within $\pm 0.05\%$ of GDP across S1, S2, and S3.

## 4. Multi-Level Experimental & Quasi-Experimental Identification (`M05`, `M08`, `M10`, `M15`)
- **Two-Stage Saturation Cluster Design & Commuting Mobility Spillover Closure:** Combine individual-level randomization with $J = 60\text{–}80$ spatial clusters (30–40 treated vs. 30–40 matched control units). In metropolitan areas, cluster boundaries are defined strictly along Functional Urban Areas (FUAs / *Áreas Urbanas Funcionales* INE) with internal commuting self-containment $\ge 80\%$. To identify and absorb daily commuting and shopping spillovers across cluster borders, integrate a continuous spatial spillover exposure estimator per Egger et al. [@egger-2022-ge-kenya] and Baird et al. [@baird-2018-interference] weighted by MITMA/INE's *Estudio de la Movilidad con Telefonía Móvil* ($W_{\text{mobility}}$) [@ine-movilidad-telefonia]. Statistical power and MDEs are calculated using two-stage saturation variance formulas (Baird et al. [@baird-2018-interference]) and evaluated via Synthetic Difference-in-Differences (SDID, Arkhangelsky et al. [@arkhangelsky-2021-sdid]) linked to 100% of regional tenancy deposit registries (*fianzas* in INCASÒL, IVIMA, AVRA).
- **Convex Kink Bunching Econometrics & Missing-Mass Phase-Out:** Replace unconstitutional randomized tax hikes with an initial labor income disregard ($Y_{\text{disregard}} = \text{€}3,000\text{--}\text{€}5,000/\text{year}$) followed by randomized phase-out rates ($0\%$, $38\%$, $50\%$) capped at the gross grant ($Y^* = \text{UBR}/\tau_{\text{clawback}}$). The disregard creates a true convex kink with excess mass for Saez [@saez-2010-bunching] and Chetty et al. [@chetty-2011-bunching] bunching estimators, while formalizing a missing-mass estimator at the upper concave phase-out boundary $Y^*$ per Kleven [@kleven-2016-bunching].
- **Structural Parameter Transportability Equations:** Decompose historical trial effect sizes [@vivalt-2024-openresearch; @bartik-2024-openresearch; @miller-2024-openresearch-health; @kangas-2021-finland; @jones-marinescu-2022] into permanent compensated wage ($\varepsilon^c$) and income ($\eta$) elasticities vs. Frisch intertemporal substitution ($\varepsilon^{\text{Frisch}}$) per MaCurdy [@macurdy-1981-labor-supply] and Blundell & MaCurdy [@blundell-macurdy-1999-labor-supply]:
  $$\Delta \ln h_{it} = \varepsilon^{\text{Frisch}} \Delta \ln w_{it}^{\text{transitory}} + \varepsilon^c \Delta \ln w_i^{\text{permanent}} + \eta \Delta Y_{it}^{\text{unearned}} + \zeta_{\text{rationing}} \cdot P_{\text{disp}, \text{CNO-11}} + e_{it}$$
  adjusting for labor demand rationing ($\zeta_{\text{rationing}}$) per Bargain, Orsini, & Peichl [@bargain-2014-labor-rationing] and local unemployment slack across S1–S3.

## 5. Constitutional, Statutory, and Political-Economy Veto Analysis (`M06`, `M11`, `M12`, `M13`)
- **Bifurcated Fiscal Federalism Architecture:** Strictly bifurcate territorial financing mechanisms between the Foral regime and Common Regime:
  - *Foral Regimes:* Classify national UBR as a non-assumed power (*carga no asumida*) strictly within the Basque *Concierto Económico* (*Ley 12/2002* [@boe-concierto-vasco-2002]) and Navarrese *Convenio Económico* (*Ley 28/1990* [@boe-convenio-navarra-1990]), establishing bilateral clearing algorithms within the *Comisión Mixta del Concierto* ($I_{\text{Cupo}} = 6.24\%$) and *Comisión Coordinadora del Convenio* ($I_{\text{Aportación}} = 1.6\%$) to hold regional budgets fiscally neutral without net outflows. Codify bilateral tax harmonization under Art. 6 *Ley 12/2002* and Art. 7 *Ley 28/1990* via model *Normas Forales* and *Leyes Forales*.
  - *Common Regime (15 CCAA):* Enact statutory amendments to *LOFCA* Art. 13 [@boe-lofca-1980] and *Ley 22/2009* [@boe-ley-financiacion-ccaa-2009] establishing a *Fondo de Compensación UBR* or negative *Fondo de Suficiencia Global* within the *Consejo de Política Fiscal y Financiera* (CPFF) per Zabalza & López-Laborda [@zabalza-lopez-laborda-2011], recapturing regional minimum income savings (~€1.5B–€2.0B) for social housing (`M05`) and SAAD dependency care (`M14`).
- **EU Law Worker Coordination & Subsistence Minimum Safeguards:** Reconcile EU free movement rules under Art. 45 TFEU and Regulation (EU) No 492/2011 [@eu-regulation-492-2011; @cjeu-c-66-85-lawrie-blum; @cjeu-c-14-09-genc] by establishing an objective Social Security threshold for genuine employment paired with pro-rata tapering for sub-threshold part-time EU workers. Codify a Default Non-Refundable Mínimo Personal y Familiar in *Ley 35/2006* to safeguard the constitutional *mínimo existencial* under Art. 31.1 CE (STC 214/1994 [@stc-214-1994-minimo-existencial]) for resident taxpayers during statutory waiting periods (`M13`).
- **Bicameral Veto Override & Social Dialogue:** Model legislative feasibility using Tsebelis's [@tsebelis-2002-veto-players] framework. Address the Senate's absolute veto over expenditure ceilings under LOEPSF Art. 15 by drafting an organic reform of LOEPSF Art. 15.6 in the Congreso to restore absolute-majority congressional override [@boe-loepsf-2012]. Formalize tripartite social dialogue under Art. 7 CE incorporating both trade unions (CCOO, UGT) and employer confederations (CEOE, CEPYME) [@vanderborght-2006-trade-unions; @vlandas-2019-politics-ubi].
- **Conjoint Survey & Deservingness Cleavages:** Pair CIS microdata with a pre-registered conjoint experiment ($N = 3,500$) testing multidimensional UBR packages across Spanish partisan, union, and territorial cleavages, incorporating the CARIN deservingness heuristic [@van-oorschot-2000-deservingness].
