# M16 · Capturing AI rents, capital taxation, and sovereign dividend funds

## 1. Why this matters
Under severe and structural AI displacement scenarios (**S2** and **S3**, `M01`), national income shifts markedly from labor compensation to capital shares, corporate operating profits, intellectual property rents, and cloud/compute returns [@acemoglu-2024-macro-ai; @korinek-suh-2024]. Financing UBR solely through personal income tax (IRPF) labor brackets (`M03`) becomes mathematically unsustainable if Spain's aggregate labor share contracts by 7.0 to 15.0 percentage points of GDP. Because leading generative AI foundation-model developers are foreign multinationals without physical Permanent Establishments (PE) under OECD Double Taxation Treaties (CDIs [@oecd-model-tax-convention-2017; @devereux-2021-taxing-profit]), Spain cannot rely solely on conventional domestic profit taxes or existing digital services taxes (*Ley 4/2020*, which exempts B2B enterprise software). It must establish a domestic B2B compute reverse-charge withholding architecture paired with factor-neutral operating surplus contributions and counter-cyclical sovereign accumulation compliant with EU fiscal rules [@guerreiro-rebelo-teles-2022-robots; @cazzaniga-imf-2024; @regulation-eu-2024-1263-net-expenditure].

## 2. Research questions
- **RQ-M16-01**: What combination of destination-based B2B AI compute withholding, reformed corporate taxation (*Impuesto sobre Sociedades*), and factor-neutral operating surplus contributions can raise 2.5–4.5% of GDP under S2 and S3 while fully complying with Article 401 of the EU VAT Directive (Directive 2006/112/EC [@eu-dir-2006-112-vat]), OECD Double Taxation Treaties [@oecd-model-tax-convention-2017], and Council Directive (EU) 2022/2523 (Pillar Two [@council-directive-2022-2523-pillar2])?
  - *Hypothesis:* Enacting a **Destination-Based AI Compute and Inference Withholding / Reverse-Charge Mechanism** (*Impuesto Especial sobre Capacidad y Computación de IA*, *IECA*) under *RDLeg 5/2004* (TRIRNR [@rdleg-5-2004-trirnr]) and *Ley 58/2003* (LGT [@boe-lgt-2003])—enforced by conditioning corporate income tax expense deductibility under Art. 15 of *Ley 27/2014* (LIS [@ley-27-2014-impuesto-sociedades]) on domestic withholding certificates—effectively captures foreign cloud compute providers without violating physical PE constraints [@devereux-2021-taxing-profit]. Pairing this with an origin-based Net Operating Surplus tax modeled on CJEU Case C-475/03 (*Banca Popolare di Cremona* [@cjeu-c-475-03-banca-popolare-irap]) raises 2.8–3.6% of GDP with near-zero capital relocation elasticity.
- **RQ-M16-02**: How much net fiscal revenue is generated once baseline Social Security payroll tax losses from displaced human labor are explicitly deducted from corporate surplus tax yields, and what constitutional limits apply under Art. 31.3 CE and STC 182/2021 [@stc-182-2021-plusvalia]?
  - *Hypothesis:* Under S2 ($\Delta s_L = -7.0$ pp), the gross corporate surplus tax yield of ~2.8% of GDP is partially offset by an automatic loss of ~1.9% of GDP in employer Social Security payroll contributions (*cotizaciones a la Seguridad Social*), yielding a net incremental revenue gain of 0.9–1.2% of GDP that must be bridged by broader wealth and destination-based digital consumption taxes. Constitutional non-confiscatoriness constraints (STC 182/2021 [@stc-182-2021-plusvalia]) bound aggregate effective capital and rent extraction at real manifested economic capacity ($\le 60\%$ of net surplus).
- **RQ-M16-03**: How should Spain structure the accumulation and governance of the **Fondo Soberano de Renta Básica y Dividendos de IA** to operate counter-cyclically without worsening primary deficits under *Regulation (EU) 2024/1263* Net Primary Expenditure rules [@regulation-eu-2024-1263-net-expenditure]?
  - *Hypothesis:* Replacing rigid annual capital quotas with a **Counter-Cyclical Windfall Accumulation Rule**—where fund inflows are triggered only when corporate gross operating surplus exceeds 120% of sectoral historical baselines—prevents frontloaded deficit drag during early AI dislocation. Robust anti-raiding governance—independent management by a Banco de España investment board, a statutory 3/5ths congressional supermajority requirement for capital withdrawals, and a debt-parity rule modeled on *Ley 28/2003* [@boe-ley-28-2003-fondo-reserva]—ensures an accumulated endowment of 15–20% of GDP by Year 15.

## 3. State of knowledge
Optimal automation-taxation theory [@guerreiro-rebelo-teles-2022-robots; @korinek-suh-2024; @cazzaniga-imf-2024] proves that while taxing intermediate capital inputs (standard computer hardware or software) distorts investment efficiency, taxing pure economic rents, monopoly markups, and automation windfalls is welfare-enhancing when displaced workers face structural retraining frictions. International tax law demonstrates that digital foundation models escape origin-based corporate taxes due to permanent establishment exemptions in OECD bilateral treaties [@oecd-model-tax-convention-2017; @devereux-2021-taxing-profit], requiring destination-based withholding architectures backed by domestic deductibility rules (LIS Art. 15 [@ley-27-2014-impuesto-sociedades]). Landmark resource-dividend evidence from Alaska [@jones-marinescu-2022] demonstrates that linking a transparent, ring-fenced capital endowment to an equal annual dividend anchors deep democratic legitimacy (`M11`). Under EU fiscal rules, sovereign fund capitalization must respect Net Primary Expenditure ceilings under *Regulation (EU) 2024/1263* [@regulation-eu-2024-1263-net-expenditure].

## 5. Methods & identification
1. **The B2B AI Compute Withholding / Reverse-Charge Architecture (IECA):**
   - Legislate the *Impuesto Especial sobre Capacidad y Computación de IA* (*IECA*) levied on domestic enterprises purchasing commercial compute, GPU cloud hosting, and foundation model API inference from non-resident entities lacking a permanent establishment in Spain (TRIRNR [@rdleg-5-2004-trirnr]).
   - Condition the tax deductibility of cloud, compute, and AI software expenses under LIS Art. 15 [@ley-27-2014-impuesto-sociedades] on the verified filing of the Form 216/296 withholding certificate or payment of the reverse-charge excise.
   - Align virtual presence definitions with OECD Pillar One Amount A and Pillar Two Directive (EU) 2022/2523 [@council-directive-2022-2523-pillar2; @devereux-2021-taxing-profit].
2. **Factor-Neutral Net Operating Surplus Contribution (The IRAP Architecture):**
   - Design a domestic tax base $B_{\text{surplus}} = \text{EBE} - \delta K = \text{Value Added} - \text{Personnel Costs} - \text{Depreciation}$.
   - By levying the rate directly on the net operating margin rather than transaction invoices, the tax avoids classification as a turnover tax under Art. 401 of Directive 2006/112/EC (Case C-475/03 [@cjeu-c-475-03-banca-popolare-irap]).
   - Enforce constitutional non-confiscatoriness boundaries (STC 182/2021 [@stc-182-2021-plusvalia]) ensuring effective combined corporate tax rates do not exceed manifested profitability.
3. **Double-Counting Correction in S1–S3 Fiscal Accounting:**
   - Ingest labor displacement vectors from `M01` to compute the simultaneous loss in employee and employer Social Security contributions: $\Delta \text{Rev}_{\text{net}} = \tau_{\text{surplus}} \Delta \text{EBE} - \tau_{\text{SS}} \Delta W \cdot L$.
   - Feed corrected net revenue paths into the consolidated Administraciones Públicas fiscal bridge (`M03`).
4. **Counter-Cyclical Sovereign Fund Accumulation & Governance Model:**
   - Specify the Windfall Accumulation Rule: fund inflows $S_t$ are drawn from corporate surplus surcharges only when sector-level operating surplus exceeds $1.20 \times \overline{\text{EBE}}_{t-3, t-1}$, protecting primary deficit limits under Regulation (EU) 2024/1263 [@regulation-eu-2024-1263-net-expenditure].
   - Formalize statutory anti-raiding governance: independent executive board under Banco de España oversight, a 3/5ths congressional supermajority requirement for capital principal drawdown, and debt-neutrality constraints per *Ley 28/2003* [@boe-ley-28-2003-fondo-reserva].

## 6. Pilot implications
- **Municipal:** Municipalities have no legal competence over corporate income tax, wealth taxation, or capital sovereign funds under *Ley 7/1985* (LBRL); municipal pilots must be entirely funded through external transfers or municipal budget reallocations.
- **Regional (*Régimen Común* vs. *Régimen Foral*):**
  - In *Régimen Común*, autonomous communities possess wealth tax regulatory autonomy (*Impuesto sobre el Patrimonio*), but cannot enact autonomous corporate surplus taxes or digital services taxes.
  - In *Régimen Foral* (Basque Country and Navarre), provincial treasuries possess exclusive competence over the *Impuesto sobre Sociedades foral* under the *Concierto Económico*, providing the only sub-national jurisdiction with the legal power to pilot a factor-neutral operating surplus contribution.
- **National:** The definitive level for national corporate tax reform, execution of the IECA B2B compute reverse-charge withholding, collection of the Digital Services Tax, and capitalization of the State Sovereign Dividend Fund.

## 7. Risks, counterarguments & open issues
- **Counterargument (Capital Flight and Relocation of R&D):** Critics argue that taxing corporate surpluses will cause high-tech firms to abandon Spain. *Mitigation:* The tax base combines origin-based net operating surplus with destination-based B2B compute reverse charges, neutralizing treaty shopping and cross-border flight incentives [@devereux-2021-taxing-profit].
- **Counterargument (Sovereign Fund Dividend Lag):** An accumulated fund cannot solve the immediate fiscal shock of S2 AI displacement in Year 1. *Mitigation:* The counter-cyclical windfall rule avoids draining general revenues during downturns, while initial UBR financing is carried by reformed IRPF (`M03`).
